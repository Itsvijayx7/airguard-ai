## 2. Core Pillars

### A. Non-Destructive Data Cleaning
Municipal monitoring records frequently suffer from missing sensor bursts, extreme optical chamber obstruction spikes, and negative zero-drift values.
- **Rule 1:** Missing values are segregated and counted in completeness metrics, NEVER fabricated.
- **Rule 2:** Suspicious spikes (e.g. > 1000 µg/m³) and zero-drift negatives are isolated into the Data Quality Center with explicit remediation codes (`suspicious_high`, `suspicious_negative`).
- **Rule 3:** Original row indices (`_original_row_idx`) are preserved on every single record for regulatory auditing.

### B. Mathematical Grounding & Evidence Traceability
Every quantitative claim generated by AI is linked to an Evidence ID (e.g., `EVID-WARD2-TREND`).
Each Evidence record stores:
- Source filename (`mumbai_civic_air_quality_messy.csv`)
- Raw row number (`Row #29`)
- Baseline and current measurement (`80 µg/m³ -> 108 µg/m³`)
- Calculation formula (`((108 - 80) / 80) * 100`)
- Result (`+35.0% change`)

### C. Role-Based Action Framework
1. **Municipal Officers:** Focus on street-level mechanical misting, industrial stack enforcement, and inter-ward traffic restrictions.
2. **Housing Societies:** Focus on dry broom sweeping bans, HVAC HEPA recirculation, and senior resident advisories.
3. **School Administrators:** Focus on playground recess cancellation, bus route transit protections, and infirmary asthma readiness.
